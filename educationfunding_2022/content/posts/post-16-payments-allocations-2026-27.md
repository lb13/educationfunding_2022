DfE published the [payment and allocation schedule for post-16 settings for academic year 2026-27](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/post-16-revenue-funding-payments-and-allocations/payments-and-allocations-for-post-16-settings-for-academic-year-2026-to-2027) on 16 September 2026. It sets out when providers can expect allocations to be issued and when payments will land.

The document covers all major post-16 funding streams. Below are the key points for the most common ones.

## 16 to 19 core funding

Allocation letters were issued in March–April 2026. Payments are made in equal monthly instalments:

- Non-academy providers: **18th calendar day of each month**
- Academy providers: **first working day of each month**

## Adult Skills Fund

Payment timing depends on how funding is provided:

- **Grant-funded providers**: equal monthly instalments on the **18th calendar day**
- **Procured providers**: paid in arrears on the **14th working day of the month** based on actual delivery

## Apprenticeships (non-levy)

Paid monthly in arrears on the **14th working day**, based on ILR data. There is no fixed allocation; payments reflect actual learner starts and activity reported through the Individualised Learner Record.

## T Levels

Allocation letters issued March–April 2026. Payments on the **18th calendar day monthly** (first working day for academies).

## 16 to 19 Bursary Fund (discretionary)

Payments are split:

- Approximately **two-thirds in August 2026** (September for academies)
- Approximately **one-third in April 2027**

## Free meals in further education

New providers for 2026-27: refer to the document for specific dates. Continuing providers: approximately **two-thirds in August** (September for academies) and **one-third in April**.

## Care to Learn

Claims-based rather than allocation-based. Claims windows are in **September 2026, January 2027, and May 2027**, with payments following approval.
